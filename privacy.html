Clan Jedi Counter

Privacy policy / Política de privacidad

Last updated: 2 September 2026. Fan-made companion for Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. Not affiliated with Moonton.

English

Clan Jedi Counter is a local draft assistant. We do not create accounts, and we do not sell personal data.

  • On your device: language, draft picks, and cached catalog stay in your browser storage. Clearing site data deletes them.
  • Network: the app may fetch public patch notes, portraits, and upcoming-skin listings from MLBB Wiki / Liquipedia so the catalog stays current. Those requests are not used to build an advertising profile.
  • No sign-in, no tracking SDK, no sale of data to third parties.
  • Children: the app is a strategy tool. Do not submit personal information about children.
  • Contact: use the support email you list on Google Play for this app.

Game names, heroes, and artwork belong to their owners (Moonton / related licensors). This app is an unofficial fan tool.

  • Menores: es una herramienta de estrategia. No envíes datos personales de menores.
  • Contacto: usa el correo de soporte que publiques en Google Play.

Nombres, héroes y arte pertenecen a sus dueños (Moonton y licenciantes). Esta app es un fan tool no oficial.
